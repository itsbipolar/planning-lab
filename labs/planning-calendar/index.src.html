      Sources and assumptions

      Every date on this page comes from a rule in the verified facts file:

        The twelve months start on this page’s own date, from your device’s clock. A corporation’s year-end is December unless you pick another month under “Fine-tune your household”, and it is taken as the last day of that month; a trust is shown with a December year-end. When a share sale counts for tax at year-end (the trade date or the settlement date) is not in the facts file, so only the superficial-loss period is shown. Due dates that fall on a weekend or holiday can move; the dates here are as the sources state them. A date whose rule is not confirmed in the facts file yet is listed as not confirmed and left out of the calendar file. General rules only, for education; not advice for anyone’s own situation.
